What Bag Builders Exchange is

Bag Builders Exchange is a market-viewing and scanner platform designed for speed, clarity, and readable market context.

The platform focuses on live SPY and QQQ views, Board Scale tools, and focused scanners that help members move from broad-market context to opportunity discovery without an overloaded interface.

The current product does not include or use a Swing Scanner.

Current tools

SPY Live Chart

Provides broad-market context for trend, structure, and directional behavior during the trading session.

QQQ Live Chart

Provides focused Nasdaq-market context with a clean view of price movement and session structure.

QQQ and SPY Board Scale

Adds deeper market-analysis views for members who need more context than the standard live-chart pages.

Impulse Scanner

Focuses on names that are actively moving and attracting attention for faster intraday discovery.

Leap Scanner

Surfaces names showing stronger expansion potential or broader move development.

Data and availability

Product availability and data freshness may vary by market session, tool, or data source. Certain modules are most useful during regular U.S. market hours.

Outside regular hours, a page may display the latest available session data, a delayed snapshot, or an unavailable state depending on the product.

Regular market hours referenced on the platform are Monday through Friday, 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET.

Important note

Bag Builders Exchange is provided for educational and informational purposes only. It does not place trades, execute brokerage activity, or provide personalized investment advice.

Members are responsible for their own decisions, risk management, and verification of any market information shown by the platform.
