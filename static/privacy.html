Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how Bag Builders Exchange may collect, use, and protect information related to use of the site.

Information we collect

When you create or use a Bag Builders Exchange account, we may collect information you provide directly, including your full name, username, email address, city, state, and account credentials.

We may also collect membership and account information, including your subscription plan, trial status, account status, access permissions, billing identifiers, and subscription status.

The platform may also collect basic usage information, browser or device information, IP address, diagnostic logs, and information voluntarily provided through support requests or other contact channels.

How we use information

We may use information to create and maintain user accounts, authenticate users, provide access to platform tools, administer trials and memberships, process subscription-related activity, operate the platform, improve product performance, respond to support requests, monitor errors, prevent misuse, and protect the service.

Account authentication and cookies

Bag Builders Exchange uses cookies and similar technologies to keep users signed in, maintain account sessions, support security features, and understand site activity. Session cookies may be used across Bag Builders Exchange subdomains where necessary to provide authenticated access to platform tools.

You can typically control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling required session cookies may prevent account login or access to authenticated features.

Payments and third-party services

Bag Builders Exchange may rely on third-party providers for payment processing, hosting, infrastructure, analytics, communications, and other operational services.

Payment-card information may be collected and processed directly by our payment provider rather than stored by Bag Builders Exchange. We may receive and retain related billing identifiers, subscription status, plan information, and transaction-related records needed to administer your membership.

How information is shared

We may share information with service providers that process data on our behalf as needed to operate, secure, support, and improve the platform. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, to investigate misuse, or in connection with a business transfer.

Bag Builders Exchange does not sell personal information for money.

Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the platform, maintain account and subscription records, support users, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce policies.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact Bag Builders Exchange to request assistance with your account information or to ask questions about how your information is handled. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, billing, or operational purposes.

Children's privacy

Bag Builders Exchange is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the platform after an updated policy becomes effective means you acknowledge the revised policy.
